# DORA Compliance Mapping

## Project Overview

This folder contains an illustrative mapping of cybersecurity and ICT risk management controls to relevant requirements of the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) for the fictional BalticTrust Bank.

## Objectives

- Identify relevant DORA requirement themes.
- Map internal controls to indicative DORA article references.
- Document potential control gaps and remediation actions.
- Identify evidence needed to support assessment conclusions.
- Support the wider cybersecurity GRC assessment.

## Workbook

- `DORA-Compliance-Mapping.xlsx` — illustrative control mapping, status summary, gaps, recommended actions, owners, and target dates.

## Disclaimer

BalticTrust Bank is fictional and used for educational portfolio purposes. This workbook is not legal advice, a formal audit, or proof of DORA compliance. Article references, applicability, and detailed obligations must be validated against current official EU legal materials and applicable regulatory technical standards.

## Reference
