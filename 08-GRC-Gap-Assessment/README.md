# GRC Gap Assessment

## Project Overview

This folder contains a consolidated Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) gap assessment for the fictional BalticTrust Bank.

## Objectives

- Consolidate observations from the ISO 27001, NIST CSF 2.0, and DORA control mappings.
- Identify gaps in cybersecurity governance and ICT risk management.
- Prioritize improvements based on risk and business impact.
- Assign remediation actions, owners, and target dates.
- Support management reporting and continuous improvement.

## Assessment Methodology

1. Review the existing risk register, security control catalogue, and framework mappings.
2. Record each identified gap and its supporting evidence or assessment assumption.
3. Evaluate impact and likelihood using a documented risk-rating method.
4. Prioritize remediation actions.
5. Assign an accountable owner and target completion date.
6. Track remediation progress and retain closure evidence.

## Scope and Disclaimer

BalticTrust Bank is fictional and created for educational portfolio purposes. The assessment is illustrative, not a formal audit, legal opinion, or proof of compliance. Findings and ratings must be validated against evidence before being treated as actual assessment results.
