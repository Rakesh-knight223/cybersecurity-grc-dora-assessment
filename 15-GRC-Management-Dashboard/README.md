# GRC Management Dashboard

## Overview
This folder contains an illustrative Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Management Dashboard for BalticTrust Bank, a fictional financial institution used for a cybersecurity GRC portfolio project.

The dashboard presents example risk and remediation metrics to demonstrate how management could monitor selected cybersecurity governance activities.

## Objectives
- Summarize selected risk and remediation indicators.
- Visualize the distribution of risk items by severity.
- Present remediation status in a chart.
- Document metric definitions and data-quality limitations.
- Demonstrate how GRC information can be communicated to management.

## Workbook
**File:** `GRC-Management-Dashboard.xlsx`

The workbook includes:
- Dashboard with summary metrics and charts
- Dashboard Data with illustrative records
- Metric Definitions with explanations and maintenance guidance

## Key Metrics
- Assets in inventory
- Open risk items
- High and critical risks
- Open remediation actions
- Risk distribution
- Remediation status

## Data Governance
The workbook uses fictional sample data and is not automatically connected to the other portfolio workbooks. Before operational use, reconcile the metrics against validated source registers, document the reporting period, and confirm that the metric definitions and risk thresholds are approved.

## Disclaimer
BalticTrust Bank and all sample records and figures are fictional. This dashboard demonstrates a reporting approach; it is not an actual audit result, a verified measure of security posture, or evidence of regulatory compliance.
