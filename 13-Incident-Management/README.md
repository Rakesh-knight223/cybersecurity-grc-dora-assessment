# Incident Management Plan

## Overview
This folder contains an illustrative Incident Management Plan for BalticTrust Bank, a fictional financial institution created for a cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) portfolio project.

The plan demonstrates a structured approach to identifying, classifying, escalating, investigating, containing, and recovering from cybersecurity and ICT incidents.

## Objectives
- Establish a consistent incident response lifecycle.
- Define incident severity levels and escalation procedures.
- Clarify incident response roles and responsibilities.
- Maintain incident records, action logs, and decision history.
- Document post-incident reviews and remediation activities.

## Workbook
**File:** `Incident-Management-Plan.xlsx`

The workbook includes:
- Incident classification matrix
- Incident response roles and responsibilities
- Response lifecycle
- Incident register
- Action and decision log
- Post-incident review template

## Incident Response Lifecycle
1. Detect and report the incident.
2. Triage and classify its severity.
3. Mobilize the response team.
4. Contain the incident.
5. Investigate and eradicate the cause.
6. Recover and validate affected services.
7. Assess communication and notification obligations.
8. Close the incident and document lessons learned.

## Important Disclaimer
BalticTrust Bank and all sample incident records are fictional. Severity criteria, roles, response targets, and procedures are illustrative proposals, not approved operational policies or evidence of regulatory compliance. Real-world use requires review against the organization's risk profile, current legal requirements, and approved procedures.
