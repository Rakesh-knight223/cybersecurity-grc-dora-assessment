# Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Plan

## Project Overview

This folder contains an illustrative Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster Recovery (DR) plan for the fictional BalticTrust Bank.

## Objectives

- Identify critical business services and their dependencies.
- Define proposed Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO).
- Document recovery strategies and responsibilities.
- Establish recovery scenarios, communication procedures, and testing requirements.
- Track recovery tests and improvement actions.

## Workbook

- `BCP-DR-Plan.xlsx` — critical service inventory, recovery plan, and summary dashboard.

## Key Terms

- **BCP:** Arrangements for maintaining or restoring essential business activities during disruption.
- **DR:** Procedures for restoring IT systems, applications, and data.
- **RTO:** Target time to restore a service after disruption.
- **RPO:** Target maximum data-loss window, measured in time.
- **MTD:** Maximum tolerable downtime before the impact becomes unacceptable.

## Disclaimer

BalticTrust Bank is fictional. Recovery objectives, service details, and procedures are illustrative and must be validated through business impact analysis, technical feasibility review, management approval, and controlled testing before operational use.
