# Third-Party ICT Risk Assessment

## Overview
This folder contains an illustrative Third-Party ICT Risk Assessment for BalticTrust Bank, a fictional financial institution used for a cybersecurity Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) portfolio project.

The assessment demonstrates a structured approach to identifying, evaluating, treating, and monitoring risks associated with external Information and Communication Technology (ICT) service providers.

## Objectives
- Identify critical ICT service providers and their dependencies.
- Assess inherent and residual third-party risks.
- Review supplier security, privacy, resilience, and contractual controls.
- Track due diligence gaps and risk treatment actions.
- Evaluate supplier concentration, monitoring requirements, and exit strategies.

## Workbook
**File:** `Third-Party-ICT-Risk-Assessment.xlsx`

The workbook contains:
- Provider inventory
- Third-party risk register
- Due diligence checklist
- Supplier monitoring and exit review
- Risk scoring guidance

## Assessment Method
The example risk register uses a 1–5 likelihood scale and a 1–5 impact scale.

**Risk score = Likelihood × Impact**

The risk bands and scores are illustrative and must be aligned with an organization's approved risk methodology. Residual risk ratings should be supported by validated control evidence.

## Regulatory Context
The assessment is intended to support learning about ICT third-party risk management in a financial-services context, including concepts relevant to the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA).

This workbook alone does not demonstrate DORA compliance. Applicable requirements should be assessed against current legislation, relevant technical standards, contractual obligations, and the institution's circumstances.

## Disclaimer
BalticTrust Bank, all providers, risk scenarios, ratings, and dates in this workbook are fictional. The assessment is a portfolio demonstration, not actual supplier due diligence, audit evidence, or a statement of regulatory compliance.
